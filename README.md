## 🟢 Section 1: Warm-Up (Easy → Medium)

> Designed for newcomers and curious community members.

### Q1. What problem is OpenGradient primarily solving?

A) AI model monetization  
B) Centralized AI hosting  
C) Verifiable and decentralized AI computation  
D) AI-generated content creation  

<details>
<summary>✅ Show Answer</summary>

**Correct Answer:** C  

**Why:**  
OpenGradient focuses on bringing AI compute on-chain with verifiability and transparency,
reducing reliance on opaque centralized systems.
</details>

---

### Q2. What best describes the OpenGradient Model Hub?

A) An NFT marketplace  
B) A centralized model store  
C) A decentralized repository for discovering and using AI models  
D) A data analytics dashboard  

<details>
<summary>✅ Show Answer</summary>

**Correct Answer:** C  

**Why:**  
The Model Hub enables users and builders to explore, test, and integrate AI models
within the OpenGradient ecosystem.
</details>

## 🟡 Section 2: Core Understanding (Medium)

> Focused on ecosystem design and contributor understanding.

### Q3. Why does OpenGradient emphasize decentralization for AI inference?

A) To increase infrastructure costs  
B) To limit access to models  
C) To remove single points of failure and improve trust  
D) To slow down execution  

<details>
<summary>✅ Show Answer</summary>

**Correct Answer:** C  

**Why:**  
Decentralized inference improves resilience, transparency, and trust by avoiding
centralized control and opaque execution.
</details>

---

### Q4. Which activity most strongly signals a high-quality OpenGradient contributor?

A) Posting daily “gm” messages  
B) Asking moderators for roles  
C) Creating educational content or providing product feedback  
D) Passive participation  

<details>
<summary>✅ Show Answer</summary>

**Correct Answer:** C  

**Why:**  
Meaningful contributions focus on learning, testing, explaining, and improving
the ecosystem — not message volume.
</details>
